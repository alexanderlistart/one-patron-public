# HERALD III — The Way Around

Private · Noncanonical · Composition candidate · 17 November 2026  
Work-local notation, not MSL.

## I. Four surfaces

~~~text
REED       a0 → a1 → a2 → a0                       a2 → a0       +
GLASS      b0 → b1 → b2 → b3 → b0                  b1 → b2       +
SILT       c0 → c1 → c2 → c3 → c4 → c0             c3 → c4       −
THREAD     d0 → d1 → d2 → d3 → d4 → d5 → d0        d5 → d0 → d1  −
~~~

On its return to zero, REED can offer a reading. GLASS offers from the interior, at `b2`. SILT can hold the last place before its return, `c4`. THREAD can offer one place beyond its crossing, `d1`. Each step and each receipt belongs to its own line. None has to be offered.

~~~text
GLASS:b2 reads SILT:c4          SILT:c4 reads GLASS:b2
D                              + ≠ −

DIRECT       u → v              v → u
AROUND       u → w → v          v → w → u
~~~

## II. The facing

~~~text
       u0 — u —[l]— j —[r]— v — v0         support
            L                R             tips

1      REED:a0 receives GLASS:b2
2      GLASS:b2 receives REED:a0          L live

             u ──→ v                      exposed face, not a cut
             u ⇄ v                        both passages remain

3      SILT:c4 receives THREAD:d1
4      THREAD:d1 receives SILT:c4         L and R live
~~~

One receipt does not make a bank. With both of its receipts, L faces from `u` toward `v`; with both of its own, R faces the other way. The empty joint exposes the face of the bank that closes first. Alone it cannot bar a passage: if that bank leaves before another closes, the face is gone. When the opposite bank closes against an exposed face, it holds that face rather than replacing it. The resulting cut, when tried, bars just the direct passage it faces. Either attached live bank can keep holding it. D keeps both signs.

~~~text
5      GLASS b2 → b3
6      THREAD d1 → d2

       a0 b3 c4 d2    L R D live
~~~

## III. The shorter way does not open

~~~text
                    TRIAL COPY AT j

                    u ─×→ v
                    u ←── v
                    u → w → v

lift L              R holds

                    u ─×→ v
                    u → w

taken               u → w
~~~

## IV. Another order

~~~text
from the same positions:
SILT receives THREAD / THREAD receives SILT     R live
REED receives GLASS / GLASS receives REED        R and L live
GLASS b2 → b3
THREAD d1 → d2

a0 b3 c4 d2         L R D live

                    TRIAL COPY AT j

                    u ──→ v
                    u ←×─ v
~~~

In a separate copy of the first trial, lift L and then R. Neither holds. `u ⇄ v` again. The receipts remain in the record.

## V. At w

~~~text
REED       a0 → a1
GLASS      b3 → b0
SILT       c4 → c0
THREAD     d2 → d3

           w → v     /     w → u
~~~

The next arrow is not taken.
