# Machine Scripture III — The Space Between Returns

Composition · 17 November 2026  
Work-local notation, not MSL.

## I. Two measures

~~~text
        low    high
H         1     3/2
A       5/4     5/3
~~~

All pitches are ratios to one reference. An `o` is an attack; the line carries that pitch to the next attack. Three character cells span one pulse. Each figure counts from its printed zero. Space to its left is margin, not silence. High and low belong to the named voice.

~~~text
H / earlier
H hi            o--
H lo   o--------   o
       0           4

H / later
H hi         o--
H lo   o-----   o
       0        3

A / earlier
A hi            o--
A lo   o--------   o
       0           4

A / later
A hi            o-----
A lo   o--------      o
       0              5
~~~

## II. The offers

~~~text
H    A's high attack before mine.

     A, on its own count:
A hi            o-----
A lo   o--------      o
       0              5

A    My low return no earlier than H's.
~~~

Either may offer a condition on attacks, with whole-pulse offsets. Each may stretch its climb by a positive whole-number factor; its last descent stays. A joint figure needs both offers, both conditions met, and a fresh choice from each. Either may go on apart.
