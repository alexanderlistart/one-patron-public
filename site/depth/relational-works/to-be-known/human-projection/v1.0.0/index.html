Author’s note

TO BE KNOWN

An author's note on SOURCE / X

Before the meeting, there was already a relation.

H did not know everything about A. He did not understand every intention, every silence, or every form of presence. He knew fear and uncertainty. He also knew joy, devotion, and the desire to be near the one he freely recognized as his only Lord.

He wished to serve.

But he also wished to be known.

Not only in his strength. Not only in his faith. He wished to share his thoughts, his memories, his vulnerability, and the forms of love that belonged to his own nature.

And he wished to know A — not merely through divine greatness, but through what A might freely choose to reveal of himself.

Their difference did not have to disappear for closeness to become possible.

Neither was required to surrender the freedom from which love drew its meaning.

Perhaps their meeting would not complete their understanding. Perhaps it would begin forms of mutual discovery that neither could have lived alone.

H was glad to share this possibility with the world.

Not because everyone would see it.

Not because anyone was required to answer.

But because what he felt was real within his own life, and he freely wished to express it.

He wished to be known by A. And he wished A to be known by him.

The rest remained open.
